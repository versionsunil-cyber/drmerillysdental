Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our treatments and patient care.

What should I expect during my first visit? expand_more
Your first visit includes a comprehensive oral examination, digital X-rays if necessary, and a detailed consultation to discuss your dental health goals and create a personalized treatment plan.
Do you offer painless dentistry? expand_more
Absolutely. We utilize advanced numbing techniques and modern equipment to ensure every procedure is as comfortable and pain-free as possible.
How long do dental implants last? expand_more
With proper care and routine dental check-ups, dental implants can last a lifetime. The ceramic crowns attached to them may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear.
Are porcelain veneers right for me? expand_more
Veneers are an excellent option for correcting discoloration, chips, gaps, or misaligned teeth. We provide a thorough aesthetic evaluation to determine if they are the best solution for your smile.
What payment options do you accept? expand_more
We accept major credit/debit cards, UPI, and bank transfers. We also offer flexible payment plans for extensive treatments like full mouth rehabilitation and implants.
